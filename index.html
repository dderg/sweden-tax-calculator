Model notes
  • Personal tax = municipal × (gross − basic deduction) − earned-income credit + 20% × (gross − threshold, if above).
  • Dividend room (2026 rules): 322,400/yr + 50% × (salary − 644,800), unused room saved. Within room 20%. Dividends only from taxed free equity (cash minus deferral fund).
  • Tax deferral fund: 25% of this year's positive result set aside; reversed in future years to absorb the loss created by paying salary with no profit. Any fund left at the end is reversed and taxed.
  • Money left in the company at the end is valued as if paid out later: within remaining room at 20%, the rest via salary (employer contributions + income tax, taxed alone in its own year).
