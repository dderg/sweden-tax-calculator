It searches salary during the profitable years, salary during the payout years, and dividends (within the 20% room and above it) and reports the plan that leaves you with the most money after all taxes.

Model notes
  • Personal income tax follows Skatteverket's 2026 formulas (SKV 433): income-dependent grundavdrag, bracketed jobbskatteavdrag on salary only, skattereduktion för förvärvsinkomst, 20% state tax above 643,000 taxable. Church/burial fee and public-service fee ignored.
  • Salary also earns pension rights (18.5% of income up to 8.07 IBB = 673,038) and sickness/parental cover. This non-cash value is counted at the "pension/SGI %" parameter (default 10% of salary up to the ceiling); set to 0 to compare cash only.
  • Dividend room ("gränsbelopp", 2026 rules, per Skatteverket): base 322,400/yr per company (split between owners) + 50% × (last year's owner payroll − 644,800; deducted once — spouses compute jointly), unused room saved without uplift. Dividends within room: 20% tax.
  • Dividends above room: taxed as employment income on top of that year's salary (no employer contributions, no pension/benefit rights), split between owners, up to a ceiling of 90 IBB (7,254,000 kr) per person and year; anything above the ceiling is capital income at 30%.
  • With 2 owners (spouses, 50/50) both take the same salary; each has their own brackets and earned-income credit.
  • Tax deferral fund (periodiseringsfond): 25% of this year's result set aside; reversed in future years to absorb losses from salary paid with no profit; any rest is taxed at the end. Dividends only from taxed free equity (cash minus fund).
  • Anything still in the company after the last year is paid out in one extra year with no salary (in-room 20%, rest as income). "Dissolve now" = the same, done right after this year (liquidation proceeds follow the same 3:12 split).
  • "Dormant then liquidate" (trädabolag, 4:25 rule from 2026): after this year you stop all activity and take no salary for 4 full calendar years, only in-room dividends (room keeps accruing 322,400/yr). Year 5 the shares are unqualified and everything left is taxed at a flat 25%.
Sanity-check with your accountant: (1) above-room dividends taxed as income without employer contributions, (2) the 50%-of-salary room rule with a single 644,800 deduction, (3) how much you value pension/SGI rights from salary (the parameter that decides low salary vs cap salary).
