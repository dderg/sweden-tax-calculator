Fåmansbolag: salary vs dividends vs deferral (2026 rules, Värmdö)

Tax parameters

Model notes
  • Personal tax: kommunalskatt × (gross − grundavdrag) − jobbskatteavdrag + 20% × (gross − brytpunkt, if above). Approximation; tune grundavdrag/jobbskatteavdrag to match your slip.
  • Dividend room (new 2026 3:12): grundbelopp 322,400/yr + 50% × (your salary − 644,800), unused room saved (no uplift). Within room: 20% tax. Above room: taxed as extra salary income.
  • Periodiseringsfond: 25% of positive taxable result set aside each year (if enabled), auto-reversed against losses (e.g. salary > profit in a later year), force-reversed after 6 years and at horizon end. Schablonintäkt (SLR × fund) taxed yearly.
  • Losses carry forward inside the company. Dividends limited by company cash (warning if exceeded).
  • "Final value" = cash in bank + remaining company cash paid out as dividend (using leftover room at 20%, rest as salary at marginal rate).
Presets: Profit = company result before your salary. Same profits in both scenarios by default; edit freely.
